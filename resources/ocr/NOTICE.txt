Tesseract trained data (kor, eng)
=================================
These files are the "4.0.0_best_int" (integerized "tessdata_best") language data published for Tesseract.js
(https://github.com/naptha/tessdata), derived from https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best.
They are used by donotext only for reading text out of scanned pages and pictures, entirely on this computer.
